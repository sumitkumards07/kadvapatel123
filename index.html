Sacred Dwarka Dham Yatra

Nearby Attractions & Holy Temples

Our central location in Dwarka enables quick and convenient access to every sacred pilgrimage destination and scenic coastal spot.

2.5 km (5 Mins)

Dwarkadhish Temple (Jagat Mandir)

The heart of Dwarka. The majestic 5-story temple dedicated to Lord Krishna, crowned with the 52-yard sacred flag (Dhwaja) fluttering in the ocean breeze.

Aarti: 6:30 AM & 7:30 PM Main Temple
2.8 km (7 Mins)

Gomti Ghat & Sangam

The holy confluence where the sacred Gomti river meets the Arabian sea. Pilgrims take holy snan (bath) here before offering prayers at Jagat Mandir.

Best in early morning & sunset Holy Bathing Ghat
2.9 km (8 Mins)

Sudama Setu Suspension Bridge

A pedestrian suspension bridge over Gomti river connecting to Panchkui island and the pristine beach side, offering spectacular sunset vistas.

Open 7:00 AM - 1:00 PM & 4:00 PM - 7:30 PM Panoramic View
32 km via Signature Bridge

Bet Dwarka (Shankhodhar Island)

The historic island palace where Lord Krishna received his childhood friend Sudama. Accessible easily now via the new Sudarshan Setu Signature Bridge or ferry.

Half-Day Excursion Island Temple
16 km (20 Mins Drive)

Nageshwar Jyotirlinga Temple

One of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas mentioned in the Shiva Purana. Features an awe-inspiring 85-foot tall Lord Shiva statue visible from miles away.

Open 6:00 AM - 12:30 PM & 5:00 PM - 9:30 PM 12 Jyotirlinga
12 km (15 Mins Drive)

Shivrajpur Beach (Blue Flag)

An internationally certified Blue Flag white sand beach with clear azure waters. Perfect for an afternoon family stroll, water sports, and sunset relaxation.

Best in late afternoon White Sand Beach
